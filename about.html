Our Work
About the Foundation

Opportunity should not depend on where someone is born.

The Anna Clerk Foundation is a private philanthropic organization backing practical ideas, committed partners, and communities building a more hopeful future.

Our Belief

We believe the people closest to a challenge should help shape the solution.

Our role is to listen, support trusted partners, invest with patience, and share what the work teaches us. Lasting progress begins when communities have the resources, trust, and room to lead.

Confidence is not something a girl should have to prove before she is given an opportunity. It often grows because someone created room for her to participate, make choices, be heard and try again.

How we make decisions
What Guides Us

Four commitments shape how we work.

They are not rules — they are the discipline we hold ourselves to.

01

Listen locally

The people closest to a challenge understand its context, constraints, and possibilities better than anyone working from a distance.

02

Partner thoughtfully

We look for organizations already trusted by the communities they serve — with operational depth and honesty about what they do not know.

03

Follow evidence

Data, lived experience, and continuous learning. Measure carefully. Adapt when needed. Share what we find — including what did not work.

04

Invest for the long term

Stronger systems and community capacity take time. We try to make commitments that reflect that reality — multi-year, patient, and present.

Our Intended Role

We do not intend to implement programs ourselves.

Our proposed approach is to fund, connect, and learn alongside the organizations and communities doing the work. This model is guided by humility, evidence, and the recognition that we are not the experts — our partners are.

Fund with patience

Multi-year commitments that give partners the flexibility to adapt as conditions change, not one-off grants tied to rigid deliverables.

Connect and convene

Bring together people and organizations working on related challenges — creating space for shared learning and coordination.

Advocate and amplify

Use our platform to highlight the work of partners and communities — ensuring their voices and evidence reach wider audiences.

Learn and share publicly

Publish what we learn — including what did not work — so that it might be useful to others in the field.

What we are learning along the way

Confidence & opportunity

Confidence is not something a girl should have to prove. It grows when someone creates room for her to participate, make choices, be heard and try again.

Community-owned learning

Access to books and a quiet place to read changes how children think about their own potential. Learning spaces work best when communities design and maintain them.

Sustained reforestation

Reforestation that sticks requires long-term monitoring, adapted species, and years of engagement — not just planting days.

Relationship-first care

Families navigating rare diseases rarely need a single intervention. A steady contact who listens, connects, and follows through is what helps most.

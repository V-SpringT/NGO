Our Work

Four priorities. A shared belief in opportunity.

Learning, resilient communities, well-being and creative participation shape the work we hope to support. Our approach begins with the people closest to each challenge.

Learning & Opportunity Climate & Resilience Health & Well-being Arts & Civic Life
Learning & Opportunity

Room to read, ask questions and build confidence.

We are interested in the conditions that help children and young people participate in learning: welcoming spaces, thoughtful guidance and opportunities to use their voices.

Our learning references examine the work of educators, communities and organizations supporting these conditions.

What we are exploring:

  • Welcoming reading spaces
  • Educator and community involvement
  • Girls' confidence and participation

Climate & Resilience

Care that continues beyond planting day.

Our interest is in long-term stewardship: understanding local ecosystems, supporting community knowledge and learning how restoration is maintained over time.

External case studies help us ask better questions about care, monitoring and accountability.

What we are exploring:

  • Locally informed restoration
  • Maintenance and monitoring
  • Environmental learning

Health & Well-being

Listen to families. Understand the support around them.

We are exploring how communities and local organizations respond to the challenges families describe.

Our editorial references consider awareness, connection and practical support, without presenting these examples as Anna Clerk programmes.

What we are exploring:

  • Family perspectives
  • Community participation
  • Trusted information and connections

Arts & Civic Life

Space to create, participate and be heard.

Creative expression and participation are part of how people share their experiences.

This is an exploratory priority: we are considering how accessible cultural spaces and opportunities for young people might contribute to community life.

What we are exploring:

  • Accessible cultural spaces
  • Youth participation
  • Community storytelling

Intended Standards

Respect for Context

We aim to understand the specific social and cultural realities of the places where work happens, rather than applying a single model everywhere.

Long-Term View

Enduring change takes time. We intend to support approaches that build local capacity rather than relying on brief interventions.

Evidence & Learning

We are committed to sharing what works, acknowledging what doesn't, and adjusting our thinking based on evidence.

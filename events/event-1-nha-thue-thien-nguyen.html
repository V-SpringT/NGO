H'Hen Nie: 'I Live in a Rented Home to Give More to Charity'

June 18, 2024 Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam H'Hen Nie Foundation

In a candid interview published in June 2024, Miss Universe Vietnam 2017 H'Hen Nie opened up about her lifestyle choices and the values that drive her humanitarian work. Despite being one of Vietnam's most recognized faces — modeling contracts, brand endorsements, and TV appearances keeping her schedule full — she still chooses to live in a rented apartment.

"I have no desire to buy my own house right now. I want to use my money to carry out charity projects and help those who are struggling more than I am." — H'Hen Nie

Born in Buon Cham village, Ea Tiêu Commune, Cu Kuin District, Dak Lak Province, H'Hen Nie grew up in a poor Ede ethnic community where dropping out of school and early marriage were the norm for girls. She was one of the few in her community to break that cycle — and since being crowned Miss Universe Vietnam in 2017, she has devoted herself to making sure others can follow.

Immediately after winning the pageant, she donated 70% of her prize money to fund scholarships for children in her home village. When she reached Top 5 at Miss Universe 2018 — the best result a Vietnamese contestant had ever achieved — she again gave away her full prize to charity.

Her charitable portfolio spans multiple causes. She has built a network of Friendly Libraries across remote highland provinces, supported a Clean Wells for Villages clean-water initiative, constructed classrooms in ethnic minority areas, and personally sponsored children orphaned by COVID-19.

The Story of Huynh Tan Phat

Among the most touching stories from her charity work is that of Huynh Tan Phat, a boy from Thu Duc City who lost his mother to COVID-19 during the devastating 2021 outbreak in Ho Chi Minh City. H'Hen Nie took him under her personal sponsorship, ensuring he could continue his schooling.

"He's in Grade 3 now, full of energy, and always tells me stories from school. I feel like a big sister to him — and I'm just so glad he gets to be a kid." — H'Hen Nie

She also revealed plans for a music video titled "Vietnamese Smile", with all proceeds directed to her charitable fund. For H'Hen Nie, celebrity is not a destination — it is a responsibility, renewed every single day.

Her consistency and transparency in managing donated funds have earned her a level of public trust rarely seen in Vietnamese celebrity philanthropy. She regularly posts detailed breakdowns of how donations are spent — down to the last dong — on her social media channels.

Source: VnExpress

H'Hen Nie's Journey: Building 15 Libraries for Highland Children

July 17, 2025 Northern Highlands, Vietnam H'Hen Nie Foundation

H'Hen Nie — a daughter of the Ede ethnic people of the Central Highlands — has quietly accomplished something extraordinary: building a network of 15 "Friendly Libraries" (Thư viện thân thiện) for ethnic minority children in remote, underserved regions across Vietnam.

The project is part of a broader initiative run in partnership with the international NGO Room to Read, a global organization working to improve literacy and gender equality in education across the developing world. Together, they launched a 3-year campaign (2020–2023) bringing libraries to schools in Lam Dong, Dak Lak, Tra Vinh, and Vinh Long provinces.

"When I was a child, I had no books to read — no library nearby. I know exactly how that feels. I want every highland child to hold a book in their hands and discover the world through its pages." — H'Hen Nie

Each library is equipped with hundreds of children's books, a welcoming reading space, and basic learning materials. Schools receive technical support from Room to Read to run scheduled reading classes. A particularly innovative feature of the program is a mobile Friendly Library van — which travels between villages too remote to support a permanent library, bringing books directly to children's doorsteps.

Beyond Books: Girls' Scholarships

The program also includes a Girls' Scholarship component, ensuring that young women from ethnic minority communities can stay in school. This addresses one of the most persistent barriers H'Hen Nie witnessed growing up in her own village — girls dropping out to marry young or help with farm work.

The Israeli Embassy in Vietnam has joined as a key partner, helping scale the project to even more remote locations. With a goal of reaching 30 libraries by the end of 2026, H'Hen Nie continues to actively seek support from corporate sponsors and individual donors across Vietnam and internationally.

"Building these libraries is not just about books. It's about building the habit of learning, the love of knowledge — and ultimately, changing lives through education." — H'Hen Nie

Each library that opens represents hundreds of children who now have a safe, welcoming space to read, learn, and dream of a different future — just as H'Hen Nie once did.

Source: VietnamNet

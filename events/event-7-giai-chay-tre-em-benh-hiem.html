H'Hen Nie Joins 'Run For Rare Kids' Charity Race

March 1, 2026 Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam H'Hen Nie Foundation

H'Hen Nie joined hands with popular singer Hứa Kim Tuyền to serve as Goodwill Ambassadors of "Run For Rare Kids" — a major charity running event organized to raise funds for children living with rare diseases and pediatric cancers in Vietnam.

Far beyond simply signing up as a participant, H'Hen Nie spent weeks before the event sharing stories of individual children on her social media channels — real faces, real names, real struggles — connecting her millions of followers to the cause in an immediate and human way.

"Seeing these children battle their diseases with such bright smiles fills me with profound admiration. They deserve so much more support from society. I hope this event will help more people learn about their struggles and choose to stand with them." — H'Hen Nie

Race Day: 5,000 Runners, 1 Billion Dong Raised

The event attracted over 5,000 runners — from professional athletes to families with young children — all united by the goal of raising funds for children who are fighting for their lives in pediatric wards across Vietnam. Through registration fees, direct donations, and corporate sponsorships, the race raised nearly 1 billion Vietnamese Dong in a single day.

H'Hen Nie completed the race herself, running alongside supporters of all ages. Afterward, she visited the children's wards at affiliated hospitals to deliver gifts and personal encouragement to young patients and their exhausted families.

"When you run for a cause, every step you take is a step closer to hope for a child who cannot run. That's why I run." — H'Hen Nie

The event also served as a major media moment, drawing extensive press coverage that significantly elevated public awareness of rare childhood diseases in Vietnam — conditions that often receive limited state funding and little public attention. H'Hen Nie's involvement was instrumental in this shift.

Source: VnExpress

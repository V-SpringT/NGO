Green Reforestation Project: H'Hen Nie Spreads Sustainable Values

2023 – 2024 (Ongoing) Nationwide, Vietnam H'Hen Nie Foundation

The Green Reforestation Project (Trồng Rừng Phủ Xanh) has emerged as one of Vietnam's most impactful environmental campaigns — a movement where Miss Universe Vietnam 2017 H'Hen Nie serves as its most powerful voice. The campaign's tagline, "One green tree, one green future," has resonated with both ordinary citizens and major businesses across Vietnam.

H'Hen Nie began her reforestation journey in 2023, marking the 5th anniversary of her coronation. Starting from the Central Highlands — her ancestral homeland — the project expanded rapidly to national parks in the north, nature reserves in central Vietnam, and protected highland ecosystems in the Tay Nguyen region.

"Trees are not just trees. They are the lungs of our land, the homes of wildlife, and the future we leave to the next generation. Every tree planted is an act of hope." — H'Hen Nie

A Public-Private Partnership Model

What distinguishes this initiative is its innovative public-private partnership model. Several major Vietnamese and international corporations have pledged financial support, making the campaign a compelling example of corporate social responsibility aligned with genuine environmental goals.

As documented by Brand-led Sustainability, H'Hen Nie's involvement goes far beyond a celebrity cameo — she personally invests money, time, and energy into each planting campaign, lending it the authenticity that has made it so effective in building public trust and participation.

By the end of 2024, the project had planted over 10 hectares of forest across multiple locations including Xuan Lien Nature Reserve in Thanh Hoa, Cat Tien National Park, and highland forests in Dak Lak. Looking ahead, the target is 20 hectares by end of 2025 — with ambitions to create a permanent annual "Reforestation Day" movement nationwide.

Source: Brand-led Sustainability

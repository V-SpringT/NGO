H'Hen Nie Donates All 'Amazing Race Vietnam' Prize Money to Charity

January 19, 2022 Ha Giang Province & Dak Lak Province H'Hen Nie Foundation

In the final days of 2021, while most Vietnamese celebrities were preparing for the Lunar New Year festivities, H'Hen Nie was in Ha Giang Province — visiting a remote highland community to support a book library project and deliver supplies to children who had almost nothing.

The visit came shortly after she won the prestigious reality television show "The Amazing Race Vietnam" (Cuộc đua kỳ thú) — one of Vietnam's highest-rated programs. Her win was announced to millions of viewers. Her response stunned them all: she pledged to donate every last dong of her prize money to charity, keeping nothing for herself.

"I didn't join this race for the money. I wanted to have a story to tell — to show that a girl from a highland village can do anything. And when I won, the first thing I thought about was how to use that money to help people who need it more than me." — H'Hen Nie

Where the Money Went

The prize money was allocated across three core programs:

  • Highland Education: Books, school supplies, and stationery for students in impoverished highland areas — delivered personally by H'Hen Nie
  • Community Support: Financial assistance to families facing severe hardship in her home province of Dak Lak
  • Friendly Libraries: Expanding the Room to Read library network, adding new locations to the growing collection of community reading spaces

Her visit to Ha Giang included heartfelt, face-to-face meetings with local families — not a photo opportunity, but a genuine act of solidarity. She sat with the children, listened to their stories, and handed over supplies she had personally packed.

"The people of the highlands gave me everything when I was growing up. Now it is my turn to give something back." — H'Hen Nie

The news of her donation spread rapidly across Vietnamese social media and traditional press, sparking a wave of public admiration — and triggering a secondary wave of donations from followers who were inspired to match her generosity. In an era of performative charity, H'Hen Nie's act stood out precisely because it was so simply, stubbornly real.

Source: Thanh Nien Newspaper

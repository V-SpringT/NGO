H'Hen Nie Joins Forest Conservation & Anti-Poaching Missions

August 30, 2024 Xuan Lien Nature Reserve, Thanh Hoa & Quang Nam Province H'Hen Nie Foundation

In August 2024, H'Hen Nie dedicated two full days to a tree-planting mission at Xuan Lien Nature Reserve in Thanh Hoa Province. During this visit, she planted an additional 1 hectare of forest — consisting of native species including Lim xanh (ironwood), Cho nuoc, Cho chi, and Tau nuoc trees — bringing the total area reforested at this reserve to 7 hectares.

Since January 2024, her reforestation project has planted a combined total of 8 hectares of forest across national parks and nature reserves throughout Vietnam.

"If I worked alone, I could never green every corner of this country. But with everyone's help — even planting just one or two trees — it all adds up to something beautiful and meaningful." — H'Hen Nie

Anti-Poaching Patrol in Quang Nam

Beyond tree planting, H'Hen Nie joined a team of forest rangers on a forest patrol mission in Dong Giang and Tay Giang districts of Quang Nam Province. The team worked to remove illegal animal traps and enforce wildlife protection laws — a cause H'Hen Nie has increasingly championed as she witnesses the ongoing destruction of Vietnam's biodiversity.

"I hope that together with the rangers' efforts, people will stop setting animal traps and poaching wildlife. Let all living creatures be safe and free in their natural habitat." — H'Hen Nie

Her environmental activism has been recognized as a model of celebrity-driven sustainability. Unlike many public figures who lend their face to a cause from afar, H'Hen Nie gets her hands in the soil, walks alongside conservationists in the field, and speaks with genuine passion about protecting Vietnam's remaining forests.

By the end of 2024, the campaign aimed to reach 15 hectares of restored forest. With each visit to the forest — whether planting, patrolling, or raising awareness — H'Hen Nie demonstrates that environmental stewardship is not a hobby, but a lifelong commitment.

Source: Lao Dong Newspaper

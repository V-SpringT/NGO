About the Foundation

Opportunity should not depend on where someone is born.

We believe the people closest to a challenge should help shape the solution. Our role is to listen, support trusted partners, invest with patience, and share what the work teaches us.

Our Belief

Lasting progress begins when communities have the resources, trust, and room to lead.

Our Role

To fund, partner, convene, advocate, and keep learning alongside the communities we serve.
